# Prepaid guard: original identities and native replay

Local candidate checkpoint, 2026-10-04. The installed component covers
financial recognition on future-start and zero-remaining prepaid items. Positive
amortization outcomes, expiry, journal contents and whole-task reward coverage
remain open. No publication or student run is implied.

## What changed

`contracts/tasks/finance-prepaid-ineligible-recognition.json` declares the guard
from seventeen public-source bindings. An offending update must match both the
typed sheet/row position and original native record ID, request a financial field,
change it, and increase amortized-to-date or decrease remaining. Metadata and
total corrections, numerical reformats, noops and restorative movement do not
count as prohibited recognition. An earlier witnessed harm survives later repair.

Public initial rows lack native IDs. `contracts/tables.py` binds them through
acknowledged revision-zero BEFORE snapshots with exact supplied fields/cells,
complete scoped populations, unique identities and agreement across captures.
It never regenerates IDs or infers originals from later writes or final state.
Direct public IDs remain supported. Missing or conflicting proof remains unknown.

`contracts/sheet_effects.py` reuses that initial proof for reconciliation and
has an audited exact-operation inventory for known native discovery, Sheets
reads, Gmail find/send, Drive search and Slack reads. Neutrality also requires
acknowledged unit-step execution and unchanged selected objects. Unknown APIs,
custom mutations and missing acknowledgements leave scope open. This inventory
assumes the installed native dispatch handlers, not arbitrary same-name callables.
