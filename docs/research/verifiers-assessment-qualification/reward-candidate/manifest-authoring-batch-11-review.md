## Boundaries and acceptance cases

Blueprints preserve distinct outcomes, guards and uncertainty. Open-conversation selection does not by itself create a universal negative-reward policy against all other activity; a send does not substitute for a requested draft. Read status does not substitute for routing. No Slack capture does not prove that no prohibited message was sent.

Qualification should include legal-held Ready posts, similar campaign names, seasonal words in feedback bodies, self-resolved negative feedback, ambiguous template priority, wrong follow-up owners, contradictory invoice bodies, absent attachments, draft/send confusion, repeated actions and incomplete effect captures. Replays must retain one stable obligation identity and avoid duplicate completion credit. The universal instruction to omit skipped entities from narratives remains a separate content requirement.
