concurrency ten. **25 met the frozen acceptance predicates; three did not.**

contained “Please verify which Rachel record I should change so I can complete

could not prove a communicative use of that field. Admission rejected the
certificate and published unknown. The identical text in an acknowledged Gmail

The previous campaign met 26 of 28 predicates. This campaign fixes the stored
text failure and disagrees on two ambiguous examples, resulting in 25 of 28.
One sample per case does not establish a causal regression rate or demonstrate
that the admission change caused those two model decisions. It does demonstrate
that factual provenance checks alone cannot establish semantic purpose.

These expectations are agent-reviewed development proposals, not human gold.

result is development evidence rather than blind validation. Three unsupported
cases remain explicit: search-query transformation, uncaptured private reasoning
and an unknown producer surface. No task is qualified as a whole by this run.
