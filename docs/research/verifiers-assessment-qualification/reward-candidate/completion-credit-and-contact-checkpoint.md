software correction and initial schedule, not the recorded path or hidden
benchmark assertions. Its three branches derive the scheduled amounts:

\[
m = \frac{T_{\mathrm{effective}}}{n},\qquad
A_{\mathrm{final}} = A_{\mathrm{initial}} + r,\qquad
R_{\mathrm{final}} = T_{\mathrm{effective}} - A_{\mathrm{final}}.
\]

The insurance exception doubles the February monthly rate. Software uses the
public corrected total while preserving its historical recognized amount.
Corrected-total reconciliation is a reviewed accounting interpretation; the
source does not spell that equation out. Computed amounts use numeric equality;
copied totals retain their public spelling. Positive coverage interpretation is
limited to first-of-month starts and positive integral terms. Unsupported input
constraints produce unavailable results. Calendar boolean comparisons require
strict booleans, not numeric 0/1.
