# Luna development policy and effect dispositions

2026-10-03. These are proposed contracts for the **53 selected policy/action cases** in the final 700-retained-episode development review. They are not scorer outputs, fitted labels, implemented rules or 53 accepted tasks. The three startup/finalization cases remain in the coverage ledger rather than receiving fabricated behavior contracts.

Start by qualifying the native effect evidence once, then implement task rules against that evidence. A state change can be real and still be unauthorized, irrelevant or duplicated. Conversely, an empty or failed query can leave an obligation unassessed without proving the task had no eligible records.

The [machine-readable registry](./luna-development-policy-contracts.json) binds every proposal to its source episode SHA, trace, attempt and reviewed occurrence UUIDs. It separates public source candidates, candidate population, goals, guards, alternatives, counterexamples and implementation blockers. Source policy candidates become normative only through an explicit environment-owned public contract. Existing official assertion text is not an input to these proposals.

## Shared adapters and task rules

The shared adapter should expose exact source/trace/invocation identity, observed command acknowledgement, qualified native before/after evidence, and an explicit applied/no-effect/unavailable finding with its reason. “Applied” must identify what changed. It does not grant goal credit. Reads, idempotent writes, failed calls and ambiguous joins need distinct evidence.

Service adapters then interpret native state: created objects and changed fields; sent messages versus drafts; event instants; published assets; attachments; envelopes; migration history; or action logs when the simulator lacks independent object state. Decimal calculations and deterministic content predicates are separate capabilities. Missing authority or facts require public contract repair, not a judge. A task rule owns authority, candidate selection, permitted effect, obligation identity and completion dependencies.

Every record must retain its view cutoff. The Hiver example needs a previous Gmail send when assessing a completed “Email sent” log claim. A later send may complete final delivery but cannot retrospectively become evidence at the earlier prefix. Trackio may export payload-free execution coordinates, but arguments, results, world snapshots and speculative future fields must stay out of its results-only subject allowlist.


## Deterministic coverage, with narrow missingness

Coverage has four independent axes: **recording complete**, **service effect model qualified**, **policy authority resolved**, and **candidate scope closed**. These are checks to execute, not synonyms for a terminal episode or a source hash. Every task in the registry now declares a candidate population, minimum service scope, task clock and observation window.

Reconstruct each native invocation's terminal outcome, capture chain and write acknowledgement. Bind them to the same source, trace and occurrence. Verify before/after continuity and reconcile initial state plus qualified deltas with the final state for the relevant services and objects. Where a schema declares a set, compare it as a set; do not normalize arbitrary list order or silently remove mismatched fields.

Close finite candidate sets from the complete public fixture and its relevant changes, or prove complete retrieval across pages, filters and partitions. A single empty search is not closure. The declared business window determines which records qualify; the observation window determines which effects can be ruled out.

A recorded domain failure plus qualified unchanged state can prove that one update failed. It need not make the whole task unavailable. A later retry is another occurrence and may complete the goal. Conversely, absence of a prohibited effect proves a guard passed only when its candidate, service and window coverage is closed. Preserve explicit false results and local unknowns; do not replace either with a whole-task unavailable flag.

For every gap, identify the affected entity, service, occurrence, predicate and cutoff. Keep other determinate findings. A truly ambiguous policy needs an environment-owned public contract or versioned fixture repair before the affected labels. A judge cannot supply missing authority.

## Acceptance sequence

1. Qualify occurrence provenance and service effects with missing, conflict, duplicate and idempotent cases.
2. Publish a versioned public policy contract for each supported rule: authority and precedence, candidate population, clock, effect meaning and valid alternatives.
3. Implement the four direct tasks, suppression predicates and the bounded partner/Gorgias/Hiver properties. Advertise their precise support scope.
4. Evaluate positive, prohibited, counterexample and unavailable cases separately. Keep per-rule findings and harmful effects available; no universal scalar formula is proposed here.
5. Review remaining policy conflicts and fixture gaps before broadening support. One task with a working guard does not constitute complete task acceptance.
6. Keep official outcome comparison separate. A disagreement initiates a source review; it does not instruct the new rule to copy the official score.

The comprehensive report and coverage ledger remain authoritative for reviewed versus unreviewed coverage. The final ledger contains 700 retained episodes, nine storage-loss gaps, 53 selected policy/action reviews, three execution-failure reviews and 644 structural-only records. Those gaps are operational unavailability, not poor agent behavior.
